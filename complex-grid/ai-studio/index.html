Emergent Urbanism

The Complex Grid, as interpreted by Google AI Studio

Three of the programs generated with Gemini in Google AI Studio, over a session of many iterations, from the growth procedure described in The complex grid (2009). They are shown in the order they were made, unchanged. Drag the Chronology slider to grow each city.

For comparison, see the same procedure as interpreted by Claude.

1. Explicit grammar

An early, rudimentary version. Each parcel is a node that lays half a street along each of its four sides. The town grows by annexing a slab onto one side of the plan, 0.618 times the length of that side, and each slab is then split close to in half until the pieces are small enough to build. The Grammar tab shows the resulting tree of parcels and street segments.

2. Chromatic network

The same growth, with streets coloured by rank, from blue for the largest roads down to red for the smallest. Roads are wider in later waves of growth, and some parcels are left undivided at random. The Topology tab lists the road segments by rank.

Open full screen

3. Modular blocks

A later, more advanced version. Every street has the same width, each annexed slab is either as deep as the plan is wide or twice as deep, and parcels split exactly in half. The Grain slider sets how often a parcel stays whole as one large block. Roads are coloured by the age of the wave that built them, alternating between yellow and the colour you pick.
